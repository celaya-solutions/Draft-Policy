What this contains

  • The water-balance math: 105M gallons/day in use, ~8.7M/day of cushion, and the 17.9% the data centers would take.
  • The power/load analysis: 2,300 MW supply vs. 2,173 MW peak, and the 127 MW cushion against ~250 MW of new demand.
  • A timeline of the El Paso / Doña Ana data-center cluster (Meta, Wiwynn, Project Jupiter).
  • An index of 68 public records — every figure on the site is traceable to a primary source.
  • Written to be reproducible: show a better source and the number gets corrected.
