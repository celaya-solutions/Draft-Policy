Chapter 380 Economic Development Agreement — Meta (WURLDWIDE LLC)

The City of El Paso's Chapter 380 economic development agreement with WURLDWIDE LLC, d/b/a Statue LLC — the Delaware shell company behind the Meta data center (project codename "Seafox"). It promises the company a grant equal to 80% of City property taxes for 15 years per phase, for up to five phases across a 35-year term, plus road reimbursement — with no dollar cap on the total.

Parties: City of El Paso & WURLDWIDE LLC (d/b/a Statue LLC)  ·  Signed: Dec 7, 2023  ·  Length: 53 pages  ·  Source: City of El Paso public records
Download PDF 53-page PDF · opens in your browser below

What this document shows

  • 80% of City property tax handed back for 15 years per phase, for up to 5 phases — with no dollar ceiling on the total.
  • Up to $7.5–9 million reimbursed to the company for Stan Roberts Ave. road work.
  • Nearly all impact, permit, review, and park fees waived; no parkland required for a 1,000+ acre project.
  • Only 50 permanent jobs required — total, not per phase — remote workers count, wage floor about $16.43/hour.
  • No water or power limits anywhere. Utilities are pushed into a separate EPWater "Water and Wastewater Agreement" that is not part of this contract.
  • The City agreed not to pass new taxes or rules aimed at data centers for 35 years, and to fight public-records requests on the company's behalf.
  • Almost nothing is recoverable if the company underdelivers, and the counterparty is a Delaware shell — the contract never says "Meta."
